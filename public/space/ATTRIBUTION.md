# Planet maps

Planetary maps by Solar System Scope / INOVE.

Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Adaptations for Physics Stars: resized to 1024 × 512 pixels and converted to WebP.

Included map: Earth day map, used to extract coastline geography for locally generated raised continents. It is not displayed as a photographic surface texture. Other worlds, craters and clouds are procedural geometry. Dimensions and distances in the landing are illustrative.

Visible attribution is included in the public landing footer.
